An original infographic summing up the world news of Wednesday 18 November 2026: Gulf escalation (Iran drones vs the US radar in Bahrain, US strikes on Qeshm), Nvidia's record $115.8bn quarter after Jensen Huang's exit, Trump in Manila for ASEAN & Quad, Kyiv's second-day strike on the Crimea bridge, Cyclone Akvan, plus the day's other stories.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the committed `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` and verifies the copy byte-for-byte with SHA-256. The image itself was drawn programmatically by `draw.py` (Pillow, build time only) — no photographs, no downloaded images.
